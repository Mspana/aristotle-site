Questions

Do I need an account?

Not to download Aristotle or to use the editor. An account is only for hosted AI, and during the alpha accounts are invite-only. A key of your own works without one.

Which AI models can I use?

Free runs GLM 5.3 Flash. Pro and Max add better models, including GPT-6 Astra. Or bring your own API key.

Does it work offline?

The editor does, always. The AI needs a connection.

Does it make the art?

It makes placeholders: simple shapes and colours, built in the editor, so the game runs from the start. It does not generate images. The finished art is yours.

Can I export my game?

Not from alpha builds yet. Export templates are coming.
