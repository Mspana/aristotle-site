When I was a kid, I wanted to make video games. I had every ingredient to jump right in. I loved video games, I loved computers, and I knew how to program. But it was so difficult that I bounced right off of it.

And it's still really difficult. We have AI to help you make programs, but we don't have it to help you make video games.

This is something I've wanted, but I haven't seen made. So I decided: why don't I build it?

Which is why I made Aristotle: an AI-native video game development platform that lets people make games easier and faster than ever before.

If it encourages one person to make games when they wouldn't have otherwise, I'll call this project a success.

-Matt
