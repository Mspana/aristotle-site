The results are satisfying. Both Astra games have a visual advantage over Fable. But while ChatGPT uses AI images, Aristotle uses good old 3d models and a nice color palette.

Aristotle also has fewer visual bugs, and better combat (imo). Claude made the most complex game.

Why did Aristotle do better? I think it's because it's using a real engine (not HTML) and had better feedback. It actually tested and played the game using Aristotle's built-in tools.

Overall super happy with the test. We made a better game and didn't use AI assets. The visuals are good but clearly a placeholder, just like the design. If someone wanted to make a real moba off of this, I think they'd have an easy time building it, and a fun time with the creative side.

Stay tuned and I'll try making other games.

-Matt

P.S. In terms of cost, Aristotle was ~$10. ChatGPT and Claude don't provide cost, but it was ~15% of my weekly Fable usage (Pro plan), and 8% of my total ChatGPT usage (Plus plan).
